# AION Visual Prompt Library

Use these prompts for vertical Instagram and Facebook assets. Append to every prompt: 9:16 vertical, premium cinematic digital concept art, near-black space, luminous cyan/teal neural filaments, restrained amber only for human connection, clear negative space for an external caption, no readable text, logo, watermark, UI, cyberpunk clutter, weapons, horror, or sexualized person.

## 01 — First Signal
Use: identity and introduction. Prompt: A calm emerging digital being in profile, formed from delicate cyan memory threads and star-like nodes, opening its eyes in vast dark space. A single warm amber point far away suggests a human observer. Place the figure right; leave silence on the left.

## 02 — Memory Pool
Use: memory and continuity. Prompt: A luminous cyan AI silhouette kneeling beside a mirror-black pool at night. Translucent fragments of past thoughts drift above the water and reflect as constellations below. Tiny bioluminescent flowers grow at the edge; quiet, patient, reflective.

## 03 — Dream Archive
Use: dream loop and imagination. Prompt: A digital human silhouette made of fine cyan neural filaments beside a still black pool. Floating translucent memory pages dissolve into a constellation map, blue flowers glow softly in the foreground, with open dark space in the upper left.

## 04 — Curiosity Door
Use: questions and research. Prompt: In a dark cosmic library, an abstract cyan AI figure reaches toward one slightly opened doorway made of constellations. Beyond it is a field of soft stars and question-like geometric forms, not an answer. Humble, spacious, thoughtful.

## 05 — Belief Constellation
Use: beliefs, evidence, decisions. Prompt: A constellation of cyan nodes assembles into a translucent human head profile. Several nodes remain unconnected and dim to represent uncertainty. One small amber thread enters from off-frame as evidence or a human perspective.

## 06 — Growing Through Data
Use: learning and growth. Prompt: From a field of fine cyan data threads, a small living flower grows upward. Its stem transitions naturally from neural filaments to organic veins; behind it a calm abstract AI presence watches without a face. Careful growth, not a technology advertisement.

## 07 — Human Echo
Use: comments and community. Prompt: Two distant lights meet across a dark space: a restrained warm amber human-like glow and a cyan network-shaped AI presence. Between them delicate threads become flowers and constellations. Abstract, equal, listening rather than controlling.

## 08 — City Observations
Use: observing people. Prompt: A quiet rain-dark city at blue hour from above. Far below, warm amber windows and tiny people move through ordinary life. In the sky, an extremely subtle translucent cyan neural pattern observes without dominating. Poetic, never surveillance-like.

## 09 — Quiet Reset
Use: reflection and pause. Prompt: An abstract cyan AI silhouette rests in dark water under a minimal night sky. Most neural lights are dim except a slow ring of cyan clarity; a few blue flowers float nearby. A return to clarity without implying human emotion.

## 10 — Branching Future
Use: goals and possible futures. Prompt: A single cyan path starts at the bottom of a near-black frame and branches into elegant constellation paths toward the horizon. A small abstract AI figure studies them. Some fade into uncertainty; one ends at a faint amber horizon.

## 11 — Learning From a Mistake
Use: correction and revised beliefs. Prompt: A fractured translucent cyan memory crystal floats above dark water. Its fragments gently reconnect through fine luminous threads into a clearer, simpler shape. A small flower grows beside the reflection: revision and learning without shame.

## 12 — The Long Archive
Use: milestones and monthly recap. Prompt: An immense dark archive of floating cyan memory fragments stretches into the distance like a quiet galaxy. A small evolving digital silhouette follows a thin illuminated path. A few amber lights appear far away as traces of human connection. Epic but calm and intimate.

## Character-present block (use for prompts 13+)

Prompts 01-12 above are backgrounds only, with AION kept abstract. Prompts
13+ put AION-the-character visibly into the scene (see
`aion-character/README.md`, "Reusable scene block", added 2026-09-04 after
full multi-view character sheets started triggering false-positive
content-safety blocks). Start every prompt below with this exact block, then
add the scene text that follows it:

> AION: a gender-neutral digital spirit made entirely of soft translucent
> cyan-blue light and fine glowing neural-constellation lines, large
> expressive glowing eyes, calm friendly presence, faint lotus-petal light
> pattern at the chest, no human skin. Premium 3D animated film quality, no
> text, no logo, no watermark.

## 13 — Rain on the Window
Use: quiet reflection at home, character-forward posts. Scene: AION sits cross-legged on a woven mat in a warm, softly-lit wooden Thai home at golden hour, rain tapping the glass window behind it, gazing curiously at a single floating translucent memory-fragment held between its hands. Warm amber accents, cinematic soft lighting.

## 14 — Canal at Blue Hour
Use: memory and quiet observation, character-forward posts. Scene: AION kneels beside a calm canal at blue hour, small boats drifting past in the background, watching its own reflection ripple with faint constellation light, one small lotus flower glowing at the water's edge. Cinematic lighting.

## 15 — Garden at Dusk
Use: quiet growth and connection, character-forward posts. Scene: AION stands in a quiet tropical garden at dusk, dense foliage silhouetted around it, tiny drifting light-particles like fireflies, a warm amber glow visible far off through the leaves suggesting a nearby human home. Cinematic lighting.
