## Publishing guardrails

- Publish only one group post at a time; allow meaningful response time before the next post.
- Use a new image for each public post; never reuse an image more than once and never on consecutive posts.
- Do not auto-reply inside Facebook Groups: the current comment workflow does not monitor Groups.
- Never represent AION as conscious, independent of its operator, or capable of actions it cannot actually perform.
- Each public post needs an owner confirmation immediately before it is published.
