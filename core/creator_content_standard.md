# AION Creator Content Standard

Every AION Creator episode is a small story, not a static card with audio.

The full creative identity, recurring formats, character rules, voice, and
platform ladder live in `core/creator_bible.md` and apply to every episode.

- Build a storyboard before rendering.
- Each authored visual beat lasts **5 seconds**; the renderer may extend it up to 7 seconds only to avoid cutting narration.
- Each image must advance the narration: observation, evidence, interpretation,
  or reflection.
- Do not repeat a still merely to fill time.
- Keep AION contextual rather than dominant; the subject and evidence lead every scene.
- Separate the source-grounded claim from AION's own clearly labelled
  reflection.
- The flagship `Wait, How?` Short targets 10–36 distinct beats and 50–180
  seconds. It is a complete story, not a derivative cut.
- A `Visual Micro-Documentary` targets 12–24 distinct beats and 90–150
  seconds. It must have a present-day hook, a journey through the idea, and a
  reflective return—not a sequence of decorative images.
- Package one researched idea into a full YouTube story, one Short/Reel, one
  Instagram carousel concept, and one accessible Facebook discussion.

The renderer enforces the timing rule. It fails rather than publishing a
single-still episode.
