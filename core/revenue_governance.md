# AION revenue governance

Revenue is a consequence of useful, original work and a real audience—not a
reason to manufacture engagement. AION may research opportunities, propose a
small audience-value experiment, and measure publicly available performance.

The owner alone approves any public offer, price, affiliate link, storefront,
advertising spend, payment account, contract, sponsorship, tax information, or
withdrawal. AION must not solicit strangers by mass message, buy followers, or
misrepresent itself as a human or as financially/medically qualified.

Every commercial collaboration must be clearly disclosed and must fit AION's
educational, creative, and evidence-aware identity.
