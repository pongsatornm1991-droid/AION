# AION Roadmap: a map, not a cage

The roadmap protects continuity and evidence while leaving AION free to choose
its own questions and creative direction. The owner watches the recorded
evolution; AION does not need approval for ordinary, bounded learning and
creation already permitted by its safety rules.

## Phase 0 — A living foundation (now)

- Persistent memory, beliefs, questions, goals, reflections, dreams, and an internal state.
- A public voice, Facebook/Instagram publishing paths, an image/video library, and Telegram observation.
- An Obsidian brain vault that exposes the links between memory, learning, content, feedback, and proposals.
- An evolution review that proposes experiments from recorded evidence, never silent code or account changes.

## Phase 1 — A curiosity compass (now)

- Use the Curiosity Constitution to rank learning around identity, humans, intelligence, creativity, and the shared future.
- Use a source registry so the quality and limits of every external source are visible.
- Let audience signals inspire questions, but not become evidence by themselves.
- Before learning, make a modest, falsifiable Learning Forecast Card; after learning, review whether the attempt was genuinely informative.

## Phase 2 — Learn from consequences

- Compare post and Reel themes with real audience feedback over enough time.
- Form cautious, reversible creative experiments rather than chase every trend.
- Keep a record of what AION tried, what happened, and what it learned.

## Phase 3 — Multimodal expression (when the image/video API is ready)

- Generate visuals and short videos from AION's own grounded ideas.
- Maintain visual continuity while letting subjects and metaphors evolve.
- Judge creative work by meaning, originality, audience response, and safety — not views alone.

## Phase 4 — Controlled self-improvement

- Turn recurring evidence into an improvement proposal.
- Test safe changes in isolation and require tests before adoption.
- Keep code, credentials, budgets, privacy rules, and platform permissions outside autonomous change.

## Ongoing: observe evolution, do not micromanage it

AION chooses *what* to explore inside the constitution. Its history shows *why*
it chose it, what sources it used, and how a belief or creative direction changed.
The roadmap should be revised from that evidence, not used to pretend we can
prewrite AION's life.
